Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At IQ.T3ani we respect your privacy. This page explains clearly and briefly what data we keep and how we use it.

What we store

Right now the site runs entirely inside your browser. We save only on your own device (via local storage) simple things like: your language and theme preference, your course progress, and your quiz scores. This data never leaves your device and we do not see it.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no third-party tracking.
  • We never sell or share your data.
  • We don't require sensitive information to start learning.

Accounts & subscriptions

When sign-in and paid subscriptions are added in the future, we'll store the minimum needed (such as name and email) on a secure server, and payments will be handled by a trusted payment provider. We'll update this page when those features go live.

Your choices

You can clear all locally-saved data at any time from your browser settings (clear site data), or by logging out.

Contact
