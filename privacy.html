Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At IQ.T3ani we respect your privacy. This page explains clearly and briefly what data we keep and how we use it.

What we store

We save only on your own device (via local storage) simple things like: your language and theme preference, your course progress, and your quiz scores. This data stays on your device.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used in aggregate (such as visit counts and popular pages). This is anonymous, statistical data and we don't use it to identify you personally.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We never sell or share your data.
  • We don't require sensitive information to start learning.

Accounts

When you create an account or sign in, we store the minimum needed (such as your name, email, and profile photo if you add one) securely through our authentication provider, Supabase. Your password is encrypted, and we never sell or share your data. The site is completely free — there are no subscriptions or payments.

Your choices

You can clear all locally-saved data at any time from your browser settings (clear site data), or by logging out.

Contact

For any privacy question, reach us on WhatsApp 0788330108 or Instagram dxq6n.
