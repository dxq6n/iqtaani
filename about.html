About the platform

The IQ.T3ani story

IQ.T3ani is a bilingual (Arabic/English) platform to learn programming from zero — interactive lessons, an in-browser code editor, explainer videos, and an AI assistant that answers your questions. All of it, free.

Why we built it

Most strong programming resources are English-only, and Arabic beginners get lost across scattered sources. The idea behind IQ.T3ani is simple: one place, in your language, that walks you step by step from your first line of code to your first project and certificate — without paying or installing anything.

Who's behind it

Mohammad Iyad Al-Ta'ani CEO & Founder — IQ.T3ani

I'm Mohammad Iyad Al-Ta'ani, a developer passionate about programming and learning. I built IQ.T3ani because I lived the hard beginning myself, and I wanted to give Arabic learners the experience I wished I'd had when I started.

Our vision

For IQ.T3ani to become the first gateway for every Arab who wants to learn programming — from the first lesson to the first job. We build the platform step by step with its community, continuously adding new courses, paths and tools.

What we stand for

  • 🎯 Learn by doing: you write real code from minute one, not just watch.
  • 🆓 Free for everyone: core courses are 100% free, no credit card.
  • 🌍 In your language: Arabic and English, with full right-to-left support.
  • 🔒 Privacy first: your progress is stored on your device — no tracking, no ads.
